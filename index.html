About Me

I'm an IT support technician based in Mexico City. I have the CompTIA A+ certification and I'm studying Computer Systems Engineering at UNIR. I came to IT from journalism. I studied Communication and Journalism at UNAM, and I worked in radio production at Radio UNAM, where I wrote scripts, planned live programs and hosted one of them. That job taught me two things I use in support every day: how to explain a problem so anyone can understand it, and how to stay calm when there is no time. I learn by building. I run a lab at home with Windows Server, Active Directory and a Linux server, and I use it to practice what a first-line technician really does: reset an account, check permissions, and follow a ticket from the first call to the solution. The two projects on this page come from that lab. I record them in English so you can see how I work and how I explain it.
